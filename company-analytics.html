Definitions

Overall conversion = distinct paid clients attributed to salespeople ÷ total recorded calls × 100. A lead marked interested does not count as a sale. Weekly paying clients are newly activated, active subscriptions plus paid commission clients not already matched to those subscribers. Square PENDING or canceled subscriptions do not count. Weekly reporting uses the local Monday–Sunday week. Subscription MRR includes ACTIVE MONTHLY plans only; yearly plans are not assumed to be monthly. Financial metrics require database read access granted to admins.
