Important limitations

Calls may be manually entered, and their saved date may not be their actual start time. Calls made outside this system, lead research, emails, meetings, breaks and other work are not captured. Long gaps are not proof that someone stopped working. Overnight calls are attributed to their starting day. Use this page for activity estimates only, not automatic payroll or disciplinary decisions. Review timestamps with associates before relying on them.
